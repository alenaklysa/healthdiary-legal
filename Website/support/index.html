HealthDiary Support

Practical guidance for protecting, moving, and deleting your information.

Email support

For your privacy, do not email medical documents, passwords, full lab reports, or other sensitive health information unless it is strictly necessary. Support does not provide medical advice.

Contact

Email healthdiary.support@icloud.com. For privacy-specific requests, use the same address. Describe the app screen and general issue without including health details.

Protect the app

  1. Open HealthDiary and go to the Profile area.
  2. Open Privacy & Security.
  3. Turn app protection on or off and authenticate with the method offered by iOS.
  4. When enabled, choose the available lock delay.

This protection uses Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID, or the device passcode depending on your device and settings.

Change Apple Health permissions

Open Apple’s Health app, select your profile, then Apps and Services (wording can vary by iOS version), choose HealthDiary, and review the categories you permit it to read. You can also review relevant permissions in iOS Settings. Disabling access stops future reading; it does not automatically delete records already imported into HealthDiary.

Hide medical details in notifications

In HealthDiary’s Privacy & Security settings, enable the option that hides medical details. Scheduled reminders will use generic HealthDiary wording. You can also manage notification previews in iOS Settings. Existing delivered notifications may remain visible until removed by iOS or by you.

Create an encrypted backup

  1. Open Profile → Data & Backup.
  2. Choose Create encrypted backup.
  3. Create a strong password.
  4. Save the resulting .healthdiary file in a safe location you control.

HealthDiary and its developer cannot recover a forgotten password. Do not keep the password in the same unprotected location as the backup.

Restore a backup

  1. Open Profile → Data & Backup.
  2. Choose Import backup.
  3. Select your .healthdiary file and enter its password if required.

Keep your own copy of important files and review restored records.

Export a PDF safely

  • Review the report and remove information the recipient does not need.
  • Confirm the recipient and destination before sharing.
  • Prefer a secure channel agreed with the recipient.
  • Delete unnecessary copies from Files, email, messages, downloads, and backups.

The temporary report is protected inside the app, but a saved or shared ordinary PDF is not encrypted by HealthDiary and is outside the app’s control.

Delete all HealthDiary data

Open Profile → Data & Backup → Delete all app data. This permanently removes HealthDiary records, imported originals, and on-device safety backups.

It does not delete original records from Apple Health or files previously exported to other locations. Manage those separately.

Still need help?

Contact healthdiary.support@icloud.com. Include your iOS version, app version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not send a backup password or unnecessary health information.
