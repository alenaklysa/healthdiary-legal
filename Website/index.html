Privacy by design

Sensitive information deserves careful handling.

  • Your records are stored on your device, so you remain in control of your information.
  • Imported medical documents are stored locally on your device.
  • You choose where exported backups are saved or shared.
  • You decide whether to connect Apple Health and which categories to authorize.
