Privacy Policy

A plain-language explanation of how HealthDiary handles sensitive information.

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Please review before publication. This template must be completed with real developer details and reviewed for the laws that apply to you.

1. Who is responsible

HealthDiary is provided by Alona Klysa ("we"). This policy explains how the current version of the iOS application handles information.

2. Information you may add

You decide what to record. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • your name and other personal details;
  • date of birth, sex, height, weight, and blood type;
  • diagnoses, symptoms, mood, and cycle information;
  • medications and planned tasks or reminders;
  • health measurements and laboratory results; and
  • notes and related health events.

HealthDiary is designed to hold sensitive information. Avoid entering information you do not need in the app.

3. Apple Health

HealthDiary requests access to supported Apple Health data only after you take an action to connect or import it. Apple’s system interface lets you choose permissions. The app uses permitted data for medical-diary features, such as importing supported measurements, body data, profile characteristics, and cycle information.

HealthDiary does not use HealthKit data for advertising and does not sell HealthKit data. You can review or change permissions in the Health app or iOS Settings. Deleting an imported HealthDiary record does not delete the original record from Apple Health.

4. Local storage and device protection

Medical records are stored in HealthDiary’s local application storage on your device. The current app applies iOS file protection to its data store. You can also enable app protection using the authentication methods available on your device, such as Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID, or the device passcode.

No technical safeguard provides absolute security. Device settings, unlocked access, operating-system behavior, exported copies, and backups outside the app can affect protection.

5. Notifications

You choose whether to allow notifications. HealthDiary includes a privacy setting that replaces medical detail in scheduled reminders with generic text. If you choose detailed notifications, health-related information may appear on a lock screen or other notification surfaces. iOS notification previews can also be controlled in system settings.

6. PDF reports and file sharing

HealthDiary applies iOS file protection to temporary PDF reports and removes managed temporary copies. When you save or share a report to Files, Mail, Messages, or another app, that copy leaves HealthDiary’s control. An ordinary exported PDF does not include built-in document encryption and may remain available in the destination, its backups, or message history. Review the destination and recipients before sharing.

7. Encrypted backups

New HealthDiary backup files are encrypted with a password you choose. You are responsible for remembering the password and controlling the exported file. We cannot recover a forgotten backup password. After export, the file remains wherever you saved or shared it and is governed by that destination’s practices.

8. PDF and image import

For supported lab documents, text extraction and optical character recognition use local Apple system frameworks on the device. Recognition and parsing can be inaccurate. You must review recognized values against the source before saving or relying on them. Imported source documents may contain sensitive information; choose and store them carefully.

9. iCloud and CloudKit

The current app may check whether an iCloud account is available, but this policy does not represent HealthDiary as providing active CloudKit database synchronization. If a synchronization feature is introduced later, this policy will be updated before or when that feature is launched, as required.

10. Third parties, analytics, and advertising

Our review of the current application code found no third-party analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking permission flow, or developer-operated server transfer for diary data. HealthDiary does not include advertising or website cookies. You may independently send a PDF or encrypted backup to a service you choose; that transfer is initiated and controlled by you, and the chosen service’s terms and privacy policy apply.

11. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in the app or remove the application. HealthDiary provides controls to delete app data. Removing the app generally removes its local application container, subject to Apple platform behavior and device backups. Exported PDFs, backups, files saved elsewhere, messages, and other external copies are not removed automatically.

Deleting HealthDiary data does not remove original Apple Health records. Manage those separately in Apple Health.

12. Children and age considerations

HealthDiary is not designed as a child-directed service. A parent, guardian, or other authorized adult should decide whether and how a minor may use the app, consistent with applicable law and medical guidance. Do not use the app to bypass required consent or supervision.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, applicable requirements, or data practices change. The revised version will show a new effective date. Material changes should be reviewed before you continue using affected features.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, contact healthdiary.support@icloud.com. Developer country: Ukraine.
